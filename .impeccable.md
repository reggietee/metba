## Design Context

Derived from the MetBA rebuild brief (see PLAN.md). Confirm or edit.

### Users
- Lawyers, law students and legal professionals worldwide working in AI, blockchain, digital assets, platform, privacy and online safety law. Usually reading on a laptop between matters, or on a phone from a LinkedIn link.
- Jobs: decide whether MetBA is credible enough to join, find and register for an event (often for CPD), find a member to refer or collaborate with, read a development in their area.
- Secondary audience: members of the public looking for a lawyer. They must leave understanding the directory is not a licensing register.

### Brand Personality
- Three words: **exacting, institutional, current.**
- A serious professional body built with the care of a modern technology company. Neutral and informational, never hype, never alarmist. Affirmative framing: what the field is building.
- Emotional goal: quiet confidence. A senior partner should feel at home; a student should feel invited.

### Aesthetic Direction
- References: Harvey (serif display, black rectangular buttons, framed real imagery, generous space), Spellbook (dark emphasis sections, thin announcement bar), Legora (cinematic photography, minimal nav).
- Structural references: Canadian Bar Association, Law Society of NSW, D.C. Bar, IAPP.
- Anti-references: circuit boards, glowing network nodes, blockchain cubes, robot hands, gavels, scales clip art, handshake stock, crypto-neon, startup SaaS templates.
- Theme: light pages with dark feature sections. Dark mode supported.
- Colour: near-monochrome, with one accent taken from the logo or a deep green.
- Shape: radius 2 to 4px, 1px borders, no heavy shadows. Motion: slow and subtle, respects reduced motion.

### Design Principles
1. **Credibility over decoration.** Every element should look like it belongs to an institution, not a template.
2. **Editorial, not SaaS.** Compose pages like a serious publication: varied rhythm, real hierarchy, no repeated card grids.
3. **Truthful by construction.** Trust signals (verification, CPD, counts) are precise and never overstated.
4. **Global and plain.** International audience, plain English, no jargon.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.1 AA in both themes, keyboard first, works at 360px.
